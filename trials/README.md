# Trials — The Proving Grounds

The `trials/` directory is the experimental frontier of Syntra Kernel — a dedicated space for
prototypes, cognitive experiments, and architectural explorations. This is where new concepts
are tested, stress‑tested, broken, rebuilt, and ultimately transformed into production‑ready
capabilities.

---

## Purpose

- Explore experimental features and proof‑of‑concept implementations.
- Host benchmark scripts, performance tests, and stress experiments.
- Provide a sandbox for cognitive algorithms, world‑model behaviors, and runtime mechanics.
- Serve as an isolated environment for testing rendering, simulation, and networking ideas.
- Enable rapid iteration without impacting the stability of the core kernel.

---

## Guidelines

- Clearly label and document each experiment to maintain clarity and traceability.
- Regularly review and retire obsolete or superseded trials.
- Use this directory to validate concepts before promoting them into core modules.
- Encourage creativity and exploration while maintaining strict boundaries from production code.
- Treat trials as disposable — but document insights that emerge from them.

---

## Future Vision

Trials are the crucible of Syntra Kernel’s evolution — the place where sparks ignite and new
capabilities emerge. They fuel innovation, deepen understanding, and accelerate the development
of the cognitive operating system.

Every major subsystem in Syntra Kernel begins life here, in the proving grounds.
