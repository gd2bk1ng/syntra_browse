# Constraint System — Values, Morals, and Operational Boundaries  
*A Phase Two Research Specification*

---

## 1. Introduction

The **Constraint System** is the formal mechanism by which Syntra Kernel defines, enforces, and reasons about:

- values  
- moral principles  
- operational boundaries  
- safety rules  
- intent limitations  
- action restrictions  
- perception filters  

This system forms the **constitutional layer** of Syntra’s cognition — the rules she must obey at all times, regardless of learning, evolution, or world‑model updates.

It is tightly integrated with:

- Axiom Seven (Constraint)  
- Axiom Nine (Continuity)  
- Syntra Language 2.0  
- Safety Lobe  
- Planning Lobe  
- Action Lobe  
- World Model Runtime  

---

## 2. Purpose

The Constraint System ensures that Syntra:

- behaves predictably  
- respects moral and operational boundaries  
- avoids harmful or irreversible actions  
- resolves conflicts safely  
- binds values to perception and action channels  
- maintains alignment across evolution cycles  
- enforces constraints at every cognitive stage  

This document defines the architecture, syntax, semantics, and enforcement model for Syntra’s constraint layer.
