# Phase Three — Continuity Engine Specification  
*A Syntra Kernel Research Document*

---

## 1. Introduction

The **Continuity Engine** is a Phase Three subsystem responsible for maintaining Syntra Kernel’s long‑term coherence, identity, and stability across time.

It is not a personality module.  
It is not a consciousness layer.  
It is a **structural continuity system** that ensures Syntra:

- remembers what matters  
- maintains stable identity anchors  
- preserves long‑term goals  
- evolves safely  
- avoids fragmentation  
- maintains world‑model consistency  
- supports multi‑session continuity  
- integrates with ThoughtStream and memory  

The Continuity Engine is the backbone of Syntra’s long‑term cognitive stability.

---

## 2. Purpose

The Continuity Engine enables Syntra to:

- maintain persistent identity anchors  
- preserve long‑term goals and values  
- stabilize world‑model entities  
- maintain cross‑session continuity  
- ensure safe evolution  
- prevent cognitive drift  
- maintain consistent reasoning patterns  
- support multi‑agent identity coherence  

It is the **identity and stability layer** of Syntra’s cognitive operating system.

---

The Continuity Engine consists of:

### **3.1 Identity Anchor System**

### **3.5 SL2 Continuity Interface**
- continuity blocks  
- identity queries  
- long‑term goal declarations

## 4. Identity Anchor System

Identity anchors define what remains stable across time.

### **4.1 Anchor Types**
- **Core Identity Anchors**  
  (e.g., system name, purpose, safety rules)  
- **Value Anchors**  
  (e.g., alignment principles, constraint priorities)  
- **Goal Anchors**  
  (e.g., long‑term objectives)  
- **Structural Anchors**  
  (e.g., architecture invariants)  

### **4.2 Anchor Properties**
Anchors are:
- immutable  
- protected  
- versioned  
- constraint‑bound  

### **4.3 Anchor Enforcement**
Anchors are enforced during:
- planning  
- reasoning  
- memory retrieval  
- evolution  
- simulation

## 7. Evolution Safety Layer

### **7.1 Safe Evolution Rules**
Evolution must:
- preserve identity anchors  
- maintain safety constraints  
- avoid cognitive drift  
- maintain world‑model consistency  

### **7.2 Heuristic Evolution Boundaries**
Heuristics may evolve, but:
- cannot violate constraints  
- cannot override anchors  
- cannot destabilize planning  

### **7.3 Continuity‑Aware Self‑Modification**
Self‑modification is:
- sandboxed  
- simulated  
- reversible  
- continuity‑checked
